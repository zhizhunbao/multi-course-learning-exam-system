## 💡 Interview Tips

### Before the Interview

- Test Microsoft Teams connection, camera, and microphone
- Find a quiet place with good lighting
- Have your resume open on your computer
- Prepare 2-3 questions to ask
- Review your project stories
- Enter the meeting 5 minutes early

### During the Interview

- Listen carefully to questions before answering
- Use specific examples from your projects
- Speak clearly and at a normal pace
- If you don't understand, ask for clarification
- Show enthusiasm about AI and technology
- Be honest - if you don't know something, say so but show willingness to learn
- Look at the camera, not the screen (for eye contact)

### Communication Tips

- Keep answers concise (20 minutes is short)
- Use simple words and short sentences
- Pause if you need to think
- Use "I" statements to talk about your experience
- Show confidence but stay humble
