The Co-op and Career Centre would like to invite you to attend the Online RBC Mock Interview session on November 27, 2025 at 1 pm.

You will be asked a series of interview questions and then be provided with verbal feedback. The hiring managers will not be providing written feedback after the event, so please come prepared to take notes on the feedback provided to you.
