# Additional One Minute Presentation - Business Objectives and Data Mining Goals

Our project has four main business objectives (/əbˈdʒektɪvz/，业务目标). First, enhance (/ɪnˈhæns/，提升) public safety by identifying patterns in traffic violations. Second, optimize (/ˈɑːptəmaɪz/，优化) resource allocation to help law enforcement (/ɪnˈfɔːrsmənt/，执法部门) use their resources more efficiently (/ɪˈfɪʃntli/，更高效地). Third, improve traffic management (/ˈmænɪdʒmənt/，管理) by understanding contributing factors. Fourth, detect anomalies (/əˈnɑːməliz/，异常) that may need special attention.

For our data mining goals, we have three specific (/spəˈsɪfɪk/，具体的) targets. For classification, we aim to identify key factors with over 70% accuracy using Decision Trees. These goals guide (/ɡaɪd/，引导) our analysis and help us achieve (/əˈtʃiːv/，实现) our business objectives. Thank you!

First, we successfully identified 55 high-confidence outliers using dual-method detection. These outliers represent only 0.55% of the dataset but require special attention due to their high-risk characteristics (/ˌkærəktəˈrɪstɪks/，特征).

This is a difference of 36.5 percentage points, meaning outliers are approximately 22.5 times more likely to be involved in accidents.

Third, we found that outliers show 1.8% alcohol involvement (/ɪnˈvɑːlvmənt/，参与), while normal cases show 0.0%. This difference of 1.8 percentage points suggests (/səˈdʒests/，表明) that alcohol-related violations are more likely to be flagged (/flæɡd/，标记) as outliers.

These key findings demonstrate that our outlier detection successfully identifies high-risk violations that warrant immediate (/ɪˈmiːdiət/，立即的) investigation and targeted (/ˈtɑːrɡɪtɪd/，有针对性的) enforcement strategies. Thank you!
